About Kidex

Our Mission

At Kidex, we believe learning should be fun! Our mission is to create engaging, educational games that help children ages 6-10 develop important skills while having a great time.

All our games are designed to be:

  • Fun and engaging
  • Educational and skill-building
  • Safe and ad-free
  • Accessible on any device

For Parents

We understand that you want the best for your children. That's why all Kidex games:

  • Have no in-app purchases
  • Collect no personal information
  • Are designed with age-appropriate content
  • Support development of cognitive skills

We're constantly adding new games to our collection, so check back often for new content!
